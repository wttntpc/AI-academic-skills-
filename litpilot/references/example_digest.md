## 3. Paper cards

### [🟢 95] Scangos et al., 2021 — Nature Medicine
- **TL;DR:** Doctors found a brain signal that tracked one patient's depression and built a device that stimulated only when that signal flared — depression improved fast and stayed better.
- **Design / N:** n-of-1; multi-day intracranial recording to find a personalized biomarker, then a chronic sensing+stimulation implant.
- **Key numbers:** rapid and sustained symptom improvement (single-subject; group statistics not applicable).
- **vs benchmark / prior work:** Not comparable to the 48% population response rate (n=1), but it is the existence proof the whole subfield rests on.
- **Relevance to us:** This is the blueprint for closed-loop MDD — biomarker discovery → detector → responsive stimulation.
- **Follow-up:** Read full text; map their biomarker-discovery pipeline against ours.

### [🟢 90] Reddy et al., 2024 — Biological Psychiatry: CNNI
- **TL;DR:** Pooling 15 DBS depression studies, the treatment helps about half of patients long-term — but blinded trials can't yet show it beats a sham.
- **Design / N:** Meta-analysis, 7 RCTs + 8 open-label, n=275, 2009–2020.
- **Key numbers:** 47% symptom-scale improvement; 48% response, 35% remission (12–60 mo); ~23 mo to 50% improvement; open-label > RCT effects.
- **vs benchmark / prior work:** This paper *is* our benchmark row.
- **Relevance to us:** Sets the numbers every new claim should be judged against; also flags the optimism gap between open-label and controlled data.
- **Follow-up:** Pin these values in the research profile (done).

### [🟡 78] Sellers et al., 2023 — JoVE
- **TL;DR:** A step-by-step protocol for running closed-loop stimulation in a real patient, from finding the biomarker to programming the device.
- **Design / N:** Methods paper (protocol).
- **Key numbers:** none clinical; describes a state-space model to separate low- vs high-symptom states and trigger stimulation.
- **vs benchmark / prior work:** Operationalizes the Scangos approach into a repeatable workflow.
- **Relevance to us:** Directly reusable scaffolding for our own closed-loop paradigm.
- **Follow-up:** Add the state-space detector design to the pipeline backlog.
